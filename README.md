HR Analytics Project

Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset. The goal is to understand employee turnover patterns and provide data-driven HR recommendations.

Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

Key Analysis

- Attrition by Overtime
- Attrition by Monthly Income
- Attrition by Years at Company
- Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Age Group
- Attrition by Business Travel
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Education Field

Key Findings

- Employees working overtime have a higher attrition rate in this dataset.
- The Low Monthly Income group has the highest attrition rate among the income groups analyzed.
- Employees with fewer years at the company show higher attrition in the tenure analysis.
- Sales Representatives have a comparatively high attrition rate.
- Frequent business travelers show higher attrition than non-travelers.

HR Recommendations

- Review overtime and workload policies.
- Evaluate salary and career growth opportunities.
- Improve support for new employees.
- Review frequent travel schedules.
- Conduct exit interviews and monitor employee retention.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Python and data visualization can help HR teams identify employee attrition patterns and support better workforce planning.

Note: These findings show associations in the dataset and do not prove causation.
